How well does AI understand Slovak?

sk-bench is the first large benchmark of how well AI handles the Slovak language. It has 30 tasks across ten areas, and most of them were written in Slovak rather than translated. In the paper we compare 55 models.

The best models answer driving-test and school-exam questions almost perfectly. Some Slovak tasks still trip them up.

Commercial and open models

Commercial models such as Gemini or GPT run only on their makers' servers. An open model can be downloaded and run on its own server by a company, a hospital or a public office.

All of the top open models come from outside Slovakia. If we want AI that understands Slovak well and keeps data in Slovakia, we need open models that handle Slovak at the top level.

We keep adding new models to the leaderboard, so its numbers can differ from the paper.

For researchers

sk-bench has 30 datasets in ten skill categories, from classification and reading comprehension to grammar, knowledge, summarisation and instruction following. Wherever we could, we used native Slovak data rather than translations. Several datasets are new or used to evaluate language models for the first time, including IFEval-SK and the Chiby and SKJ1 grammar probes.

Every model gets the same prompts through a single lighteval pipeline, so open and commercial models are scored the same way.
